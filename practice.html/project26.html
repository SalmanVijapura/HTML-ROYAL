Our Story

VeloCar Motors was founded in 2005 with a simple goal: build reliable, affordable vehicles without compromising on design or safety. What started as a small workshop with 12 employees has grown into a company serving customers across the country.

Today, we design, manufacture, and service vehicles across three factories, backed by a team of engineers, designers, and support staff dedicated to quality at every step.
